1. Who is responsible

The game Genesis Craft (Android package cz.petrkristof.genesiscraft) is developed by Wansal (Petr Krištof), an individual developer from the Czech Republic. Contact: wansalgamedev@gmail.com.

2. What data the game uses

DataWhyWho processes it
Game progress (atoms, molecules, upgrades, settings, statistics) Saved on your device and, when online, backed up in the cloud so it is not lost Your device; Unity Cloud Save

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): saving your progress, the optional account, purchases.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): personalised ads in the EU/EEA/UK. You can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): non-personalised ads, leaderboards, preventing fraud, answering e-mails.

4. Third-party services

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform: Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from apps
  • Google Play: Privacy Policy

These providers may process data outside the EU (for example in the USA) under the safeguards they describe in their policies (EU Standard Contractual Clauses / EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

5. How long data is kept

  • Progress on the device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Cloud save, account and leaderboard entry: until you delete the account (see Delete account and data).
  • Ad and purchase data: as described by Google in its policies.
  • E-mails: as long as needed to solve your request.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. Write to wansalgamedev@gmail.com and include your player ID (Settings → Contact us adds it automatically). You can also complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz) or the authority in your country.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact the developer and it will be deleted.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
